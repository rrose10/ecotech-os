# EcoTech Office Management System (EOMS)

> A modern office management system built for EcoTech.

---

## 🚀 Overview

EcoTech Office Management System (EOMS) is an internal web application designed to manage the daily operations of EcoTech.

The system provides a centralized dashboard for:

- Employee Management
- Attendance
- Finance
- Income & Expense Tracking
- Asset Management
- Inventory
- Birthday Reminder
- Payment Reminder
- Reports & Analytics

---

## 🎯 Goals

- Simplify office management
- Track daily operations
- Manage employees efficiently
- Monitor office finances
- Generate reports
- Scale in the future
